# Namma Arcana

22 cards of the classic tarot, reimagined in Bengaluru. Here, the Fool still walks off a cliff but the cliff is a pothole.

Pick your neighbourhood, ask a question, hold to shuffle and draw three cards: the Commute, the Hustle and the Heart. Then get a reading that actually knows where you live.

## What's inside

- **22 illustrated cards.** Each one follows its Rider-Waite composition. The Hanged Man hangs from an unfinished metro beam. Temperance pulls filter coffee between two cups. The Moon's crayfish climbs out of Bellandur foam.
- **A real reading engine.** It uses card positions, reversals, elemental dignities, the Fool's Journey, known card pairings and the quintessence (your hidden card). It also gives a weighted yes or no.
- **It listens to how you ask.** It reads your question's topic and tone. It won't pretend to read someone else's mind, and it notices when you ask the same thing twice.
- **Peak Bengaluru advice.** "Something will break. Back up your files before 4pm."
- **Ask the auto.** A quick yes or no from an auto anna whose eyes follow your cursor. Banni or barolla.
- **A card you can study.** Tap any drawn card to open it full size, drag it to turn it in 3D, and read its element, yes or no lean, both meanings and the tarot link.
- **City sounds.** Rain, traffic and temple bells, all made in the browser. Off until you switch them on.
- **Things to play with.** A night skyline that shifts as you move, a cinematic card reveal in gold, A neighbourhood picker with traffic and rent stats. Hold-to-shuffle, a fanned deck to pick from, and a story card to share.

## Run it

It's one HTML file with no build step. Open `index.html` in any browser, on phone or desktop.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Every illustration is drawn in code as SVG. Fonts come from Google Fonts (Tiro Kannada, Instrument Serif, Figtree and Noto Sans Kannada).
